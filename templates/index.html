How the pipeline works

Each stage is an isolated, testable component — from raw CSV to a served prediction.

01

Data Ingestion

Reads the raw dataset, performs a train/test split, and persists versioned CSVs to artifacts/.

02

Data Transformation

A ColumnTransformer imputes, one-hot encodes categoricals, and scales numerics.

03

Model Training

Tunes and compares Linear, Tree, Ensemble, CatBoost and XGBoost regressors; keeps the best by R².

04

Prediction Serving

A Flask endpoint loads the fitted preprocessor and model to score new student profiles in real time.
